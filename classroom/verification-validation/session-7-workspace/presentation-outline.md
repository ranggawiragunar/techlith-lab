# Techlith Lab Inventory — Presentation Outline

Prepare approximately 6–10 slides. Use evidence rather than decoration. Show each metric with its source, scope, and context. Highlight 2–4 decision-relevant findings; do not make a claim without evidence. Keep the conclusion within the tested scope and state limitations.

## 1. SUT and Testing Scope

Identify the system, build/source boundaries, objective, and exclusions.

## 2. Requirements and Risk

Show canonical REQ-INV identifiers, priorities, and risk rationale.

## 3. Test Strategy / Technique Combination

Summarize selected techniques and why they fit the objectives.

## 4. Key Test Design

Present selected scenarios and boundary/state coverage, with test-register references.

## 5. Coverage / Mutation / Model Evidence

Label normalized Session 4 references separately from observed Session 5 metrics. Include source, scope, and limits.

## 6. Execution and Defect Evidence

Present observed execution evidence and traceable defect records. Separate observation from interpretation.

## 7. Retest / Regression

Summarize actual retest and regression evidence; note unexecuted scope.

## 8. Traceability and Residual Risk

Show requirement-to-evidence links and remaining gaps or limitations.

## 9. Conclusion / Recommendation

Write a conclusion and next action supported by the cited evidence. Do not overclaim beyond scope.
